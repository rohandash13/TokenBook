## Recording checks

- Refresh the local app and verify the displayed estimates before recording. Refreshing signals can change Support’s estimate and the company total.
- The current October actuals are month-to-date only; do not show an October variance memo as if the final bill has arrived.
- Forecast performance is historical backtesting, not extraction accuracy or a guarantee. The current page shows seven months, March through September.
- Model Stack savings are estimated for the remainder of October, not monthly recurring savings.
- The accrual workflow downloads a CSV; it does not post directly to an ERP.
